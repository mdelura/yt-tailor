Privacy

YTailor is a personal, client-only page. It has no server of its own: everything runs in your browser and talks directly to Google.

What it accesses

After you sign in with Google, YTailor uses YouTube API Services to read your subscriptions, recent videos of the channels you subscribe to, and your playlists, and to add videos to playlists when you ask it to or when a rule you created says so. If you grant it, YTailor also uses a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive to keep your settings in sync between your devices; it cannot see any other file in your Drive. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it stores

Your settings (favourite channels, playlist rules, last used playlist), a cached copy of the feed, and a short-lived access token are stored in this browser. Settings are also saved to your own Google Drive app-data folder when sync is on. Cached video data is discarded after 30 days. Nothing is sent to any party other than Google, and there are no analytics or cookies from YTailor.

Removing access and data

Use "Clear cached data" and "Sign out" in Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser. You can revoke YTailor's access at any time on the Google security settings page.
